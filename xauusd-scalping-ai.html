LACMA GOLD SCALPING EA is an advanced multi-timeframe Expert Advisor designed specifically for XAUUSD (Gold), combining H1 market bias, M15 market structure, and M5 execution to identify high-quality continuation and reversal opportunities. STRATEGY

Advanced Multi-Timeframe Strategy

LACMA operates across three timeframes: H1 — primary market bias and trend direction; M15 — market structure, pullbacks, liquidity and setup formation; M5 — precise trade execution. It enters during early continuation moves following valid M15 pullbacks, rather than chasing extended price movements, and includes an entry-location filter that rejects trades when too much of the impulse has already occurred.

ENGINE

Trade Quality Engine

The V24 Trade Quality Engine evaluates multiple market conditions before allowing marginal setups. Scoring considers H1 trend alignment, M15 market structure, pullback quality, liquidity position, trend maturity, momentum, ATR/volatility quality, spread conditions and trading session — filtering weaker setups instead of treating every move as a trade.

SMC

Smart Money & Market Structure Filters

Incorporates BOS / Break of Structure, CHOCH / Change of Character, Liquidity Sweep Detection, Fair Value Gap (FVG) confirmation, Volume Profile POC, Anchored VWAP, trend exhaustion detection, SMT divergence, RSI divergence, session and liquidity-level analysis. Liquidity sweeps can act as a hard veto under the Trade Cycle State Machine.

ENTRY

Early Entry Without Chasing

The Early Entry Engine looks for the first valid M15 pullback followed by continuation in the H1 trend direction instead of waiting for multiple late confirmations. The entry-location filter measures the percentage of the impulse already consumed and can reject excessively late entries.

CYCLE

Adaptive Trade Cycle & Re-Entries

The trade-cycle logic monitors initial entries, re-entry generations, trend maturity, pullback depth, impulse extension, liquidity conditions and retracement requirements. When extended, the EA can switch into retracement mode and use pending BUY LIMIT / SELL LIMIT orders that expire automatically after a defined number of M15 bars.

RISK

Rejects trades when the structural stop is too wide.

EXIT

Partial Profit & Adaptive Exit

Active profit management: +50 pips partial profit, then remaining position protected around breakeven with a configurable buffer and managed using market structure. Adapts exit by structure breaks, momentum weakness, liquidity reversal, H1 bias changes, ATR-based trailing and profit-lock. V24 introduces an adaptive exit model with no fixed TP2 requirement.

SCALING

Adaptive Position Scaling

Re-entries automatically decrease in size as the trade cycle progresses via a configurable scaling factor, reducing exposure as a move becomes more mature.

EXEC

Always-In-Market Gold Trading System

An always-in-market gold trading system that trades the M15 trend directly — no waiting, no multi-timeframe confirmation stack, no missed moves.

The Core Idea

Most trend EAs wait: H4, then M15, then a pullback. By the time all boxes are ticked, the move is gone. This engine flips that — as soon as an M15 candle closes, its direction is the trade bias. The EA manages exposure as a live basket: adding into strength, protecting profit, and flipping only on real reversals.

Multi-Layer Risk

Hard caps on positions and lot exposure, position compression to free margin, an emergency floating-drawdown circuit breaker, and built-in spread/margin pre-trade checks.

Key Features

How the Engine Works

  • Immediate M15 EntryEvery 15 minutes the engine reads the just-closed candle. Bullish close → long bias; bearish → short bias. If the basket is flat, it enters immediately — keeping the account working instead of sitting in cash.
  • Winner-Only Position StackingAs price moves in the basket's favor, the EA adds positions — but only into a basket already net profitable, and only when the most recent entry is in profit. It never adds size to a losing position. Stacking distance adapts to gold volatility using ATR(M15).
  • Dynamic Basket Profit LockOnce floating profit clears a trigger level, a trailing lock arms and ratchets up as profits grow — protecting an ever-increasing floor of profit.
  • Basket Take-Profit + Instant Re-CycleWhen the basket hits its overall profit target, everything closes and a brand-new cycle opens immediately in the current M15 direction.
  • Smart Reversal EngineWon't flip on every opposite candle. A reversal only triggers on a strong-bodied, volume-confirmed candle, filtering out weak noisy candles that cause whipsaw.
  • Live Basket DashboardOn-chart panel shows M15 bias, basket direction, open position count and lots, floating P/L, profit-lock status, volatility regime, spread and drawdown.
Step by Step

The Trade Lifecycle

  • 1 — Read the trendOn every new M15 candle close, determine bias from candle direction.
  • 2 — Stay engagedIf flat, open in that direction immediately.
  • 3 — Scale into strengthAdd positions as price extends in the basket's favor, spaced by an ATR-adaptive distance, but only while the basket and last entry are both in profit.
  • 4 — Protect the gainsA trailing profit lock ratchets upward as the basket grows, converting open profit into a protected floor.
  • 5 — Take the winHit the basket profit target — close everything — start the next cycle immediately.
  • 6 — Flip on real reversalsA confirmed strong opposite M15 candle closes the current basket and opens fresh in the new direction. Weak reversal attempts are ignored.
  • 7 — Circuit breakerIf floating drawdown breaches the configured limit, the whole basket closes and the cycle resets.
